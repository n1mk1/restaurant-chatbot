# Maple & Ember Dietary Restrictions Guide

## Verified Dietary Labels

The menu data verifies seven recipe-level dietary labels: vegan, vegetarian,
gluten-free, pescatarian, plant-based, pork-free, and alcohol-free.

- Vegan dishes: Maple-Roasted Squash Soup, Charred Cauliflower Steak, Lentil & Root Vegetable Pie, Cider-Poached Pear.
- Vegetarian dishes: Roasted Beet Salad, Maple-Roasted Squash Soup, Charred Cauliflower Steak, Wild Mushroom Risotto, Lentil & Root Vegetable Pie, Cider-Poached Pear, Dark Chocolate Torte, Maple Berry Pavlova.
- Gluten-free dishes: Roasted Beet Salad, Crispy Lake Erie Perch, Maple-Roasted Squash Soup, Charred Cauliflower Steak, Wild Mushroom Risotto, Maple-Glazed Salmon, Cedar-Roasted Chicken, Dark Chocolate Torte, Maple Berry Pavlova.
- Pescatarian dishes: Roasted Beet Salad, Crispy Lake Erie Perch, Maple-Roasted Squash Soup, Charred Cauliflower Steak, Wild Mushroom Risotto, Maple-Glazed Salmon, Lentil & Root Vegetable Pie, Cider-Poached Pear, Dark Chocolate Torte, Maple Berry Pavlova.
- Plant-based dishes: Maple-Roasted Squash Soup, Charred Cauliflower Steak, Lentil & Root Vegetable Pie, Cider-Poached Pear.
- Every listed dish is marked pork-free at the recipe level.
- Alcohol-free dishes: Roasted Beet Salad, Crispy Lake Erie Perch, Maple-Roasted Squash Soup, Charred Cauliflower Steak, Wild Mushroom Risotto, Cedar-Roasted Chicken, Ember Burger, Lentil & Root Vegetable Pie, Dark Chocolate Torte, Maple Berry Pavlova.

Recipe-level labels describe the listed dish only. They are not religious
certifications, medical advice, nutrition claims, or guarantees against
cross-contact in a shared kitchen.

## Declared Allergens

The menu tracks twelve declaration categories per dish: dairy, egg, fish,
gluten, mustard, peanuts, sesame, shellfish, soy, sulfites, tree nuts, and
wheat.

- Dairy is declared for: Roasted Beet Salad, Wild Mushroom Risotto, Ember Burger, Dark Chocolate Torte, Maple Berry Pavlova.
- Egg is declared for: Crispy Lake Erie Perch, Ember Burger, Dark Chocolate Torte, Maple Berry Pavlova.
- Fish is declared for: Crispy Lake Erie Perch, Maple-Glazed Salmon.
- Gluten is declared for: Ember Burger, Lentil & Root Vegetable Pie, Cider-Poached Pear.
- Mustard is declared for: Roasted Beet Salad, Crispy Lake Erie Perch.
- Sulfites are declared for: Maple-Glazed Salmon, Cider-Poached Pear.
- Tree nuts are declared for: Roasted Beet Salad.
- Wheat is declared for: Ember Burger, Lentil & Root Vegetable Pie, Cider-Poached Pear.
- No current dish declares peanuts, sesame, shellfish, or soy in the menu data. That is not a guarantee that those allergens or cross-contact are absent.
- Maple-Roasted Squash Soup, Charred Cauliflower Steak, and Cedar-Roasted Chicken declare no allergens in the menu data. That does not mean they are allergen-free or safe from cross-contact.

## Restrictions That Need Staff Confirmation

The menu data does not verify halal, kosher, Jain, keto, paleo, Whole30,
low-carb, low-sodium, low-FODMAP, diabetic-friendly, sugar-free, organic, or
meat-only requirements. It also does not treat lactose intolerance or a broad
"nut" restriction as fully resolved by one recipe declaration. Ingredients
alone do not establish preparation, sourcing, nutrition targets, or
certification — please call +1 (416) 555-0142 so staff can confirm what the
kitchen can accommodate.

## Cross-Contact and Allergy Safety

Menu labels and declared allergens describe recipes, not kitchen conditions.
Dishes are prepared in a shared kitchen, so cross-contact cannot be ruled out
from the menu data. If anyone in your party has a food allergy or intolerance,
please tell restaurant staff directly before ordering, and call
+1 (416) 555-0142 ahead of your visit for severe allergies. If someone may be
having a severe allergic reaction, contact local emergency services
immediately.

## Substitutions for Dietary Needs

Whether a dish can be modified to meet a dietary need is not confirmed in the
menu data. Restaurant staff must approve any change, its price, and its
dietary or allergen implications.
