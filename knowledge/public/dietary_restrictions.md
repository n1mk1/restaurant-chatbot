# Maple & Ember Dietary Restrictions Guide

## Verified Dietary Labels

The menu data verifies three dietary labels for individual dishes: vegetarian,
vegan, and gluten-free.

- Vegetarian dishes: Roasted Beet Salad, Charred Cauliflower Steak, Wild Mushroom Risotto, Cider-Poached Pear, Dark Chocolate Torte.
- Vegan dishes: Charred Cauliflower Steak, Cider-Poached Pear.
- Gluten-free dishes: Roasted Beet Salad, Crispy Lake Erie Perch, Charred Cauliflower Steak, Wild Mushroom Risotto, Maple-Glazed Salmon, Dark Chocolate Torte.

A label reflects the menu data only; it is not a preparation or
cross-contact guarantee.

## Declared Allergens

The menu tracks five allergens per dish: dairy, egg, fish, gluten, and tree
nuts.

- Dairy is declared for: Roasted Beet Salad, Wild Mushroom Risotto, Ember Burger, Dark Chocolate Torte.
- Egg is declared for: Crispy Lake Erie Perch, Ember Burger, Dark Chocolate Torte.
- Fish is declared for: Crispy Lake Erie Perch, Maple-Glazed Salmon.
- Gluten is declared for: Ember Burger, Cider-Poached Pear.
- Tree nuts are declared for: Roasted Beet Salad.
- Charred Cauliflower Steak declares no allergens in the menu data — that does not mean it is allergen-free or safe from cross-contact.

## Restrictions That Need Staff Confirmation

The menu data does not verify halal, kosher, keto, paleo, pescatarian, Jain,
low-carb, low-sodium, low-FODMAP, diabetic-friendly, sugar-free, organic, or
meat-only requirements, and it does not separately track peanuts, shellfish,
soy, sesame, wheat, mustard, sulfites, or lactose. Ingredients alone do not
establish preparation, sourcing, nutrition targets, or certification — please
call +1 (416) 555-0142 so staff can confirm what the kitchen can accommodate.

## Cross-Contact and Allergy Safety

Menu labels and declared allergens describe recipes, not kitchen conditions.
Dishes are prepared in a shared kitchen, so cross-contact cannot be ruled out
from the menu data. If anyone in your party has a food allergy or
intolerance, please tell restaurant staff directly before ordering, and call
+1 (416) 555-0142 ahead of your visit for severe allergies. If someone may be
having a severe allergic reaction, contact local emergency services
immediately.

## Substitutions for Dietary Needs

Whether a dish can be modified to meet a dietary need is not confirmed in the
menu data. Restaurant staff must approve any change, its price, and its
dietary or allergen implications.
