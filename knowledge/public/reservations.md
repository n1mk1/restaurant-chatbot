# Maple & Ember Reservations and Chat Booking Guide

## Bookable Time Slots

Chat bookings use regular hourly time slots, on the hour, with the last slot
one hour before closing:

- Tuesday to Thursday: 5:00 PM, 6:00 PM, 7:00 PM, 8:00 PM, 9:00 PM
- Friday and Saturday: 5:00 PM, 6:00 PM, 7:00 PM, 8:00 PM, 9:00 PM, 10:00 PM
- Sunday: 5:00 PM, 6:00 PM, 7:00 PM, 8:00 PM
- Monday: closed — no slots.

## How Chat Booking Works

You can book a time slot directly in this chat:

1. Tell the assistant the day of the week (Tuesday to Sunday) and the time slot you want.
2. Give the name the booking should be under.
3. Give a contact phone number.
4. The assistant repeats the details back — "confirm time slot [day and time] for [name] - [phone number]".
5. Reply "confirm" to record the booking, or "cancel" to discard it.

Nothing is recorded until you reply "confirm". Once confirmed, the booking is
written to Maple & Ember's booking log with a booking number, and staff will
call the number you provided if the slot cannot be honoured.

## Changing or Cancelling a Booking

A recorded booking cannot be changed or cancelled in chat. Please call
+1 (416) 555-0142, or use <https://example.com/maple-and-ember/reservations>,
to modify or cancel. Cancellation and no-show terms are not confirmed in the
chat data.

## Walk-Ins and Waitlist

Walk-in policy, waitlist options, and current wait times are not confirmed in
the chat data. Please call +1 (416) 555-0142 for the latest guidance.

## Large Groups and Private Events

Group-size limits, private-room availability, deposits, and event pricing are
not confirmed here. For a large group or private event, call
+1 (416) 555-0142 so staff can help directly.
